This is the portfolio of Luka Gabidzashvili, a full-stack designer based in Tbilisi, Georgia, working under the name Bitz. For 10+ years the work has covered branding, graphic design, 3D, motion, and UI/UX – not as separate freelance gigs, but as one connected skill set applied to real products and real brands.

Most design projects break down the moment they cross a discipline line. The brand identity goes to one studio, the product interface to another, the motion or 3D work to a third – and something gets lost at every handoff. Bitz exists to close that gap. As a full-stack designer in Georgia, Luka builds the identity, the interface, and the visual system as one continuous piece of work, so a brand looks and feels the same whether it’s on a business card, a product screen, or a 3D render.

Luka currently serves as Chief Design Officer at Moncero, where he has built and managed product and visual design teams delivering work across Web3, crypto, and enterprise platforms, including HRMS, HMS, and hospital management systems. In the Web3 space, he led the design of Nordom Gates, a Telegram game that reached more than 6 million users. He is also co-founder of Oasis, a Web3 digital agency, where he works with both small and large crypto companies on branding, design systems, and digital products.
